About

I'm a backend developer and Informatics Engineering graduate, currently deepening my knowledge of software architecture — patterns, layering, and how to design systems that hold up as they grow.

I like building systems that stay stable under real conditions and code that's still easy to read months later. I care about the boring things that make projects survive: clear boundaries between layers, proper error handling, and tests that catch regressions before users do.

Most recently I built and deployed albasheksy.network, a bilingual store and courses platform that is now live in production. Before that I led day-to-day operations for a 6-person team at Silver Store while building its backend.

Always looking for new tools, patterns, and ideas worth learning.
